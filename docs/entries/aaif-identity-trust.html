The group's Reference Architecture sub-group has a working draft, "Agent Identity Primitives", Status "Draft", Version "0.1", dated 2026-08-27, in a Google Doc readable without a login (read 2026-10-13, open comments included). Its scope clause reads "The following are out of scope: authorization models (what an agent is permitted to do), evidence, reputation, and security controls or threat modeling." Section 3.5 says revocation "must propagate within a bounded time window"; a comment on it proposes that "implementations should declare the maximum time from revocation to first refusal by a relying party, and publish a reproducible measurement".
