Relevance

AUDIT concerns audit information about agents and delegated systems, so it touches commerce wherever an agent acts for a buyer and a seller or payer later needs a record of what happened. The charter scope reads "The scope of such auditing is the observable behavior of agents, including user intent, agent or tool interactions, invoked or declined actions, exchanged data, and resulting outcomes across trust boundaries, but not the auditing of the underlying AI model, training data, or inference mechanisms." Commerce, seller, merchant: not found in the charter text, 2026-10-02; repository example: "booking a hotel room" (issue #16).

The BoF request lists under "Architecture overview und use cases (20 mins)" a "User e-commerce use case" and names no presenter; the architecture draft has no "e-commerce" text, and its use cases include a flight booked through a chain of agents and "Financial Transactions by Agents": "Agents may execute financial operations such as payments or procurement actions on behalf of users". The sentence on VCON was proposed on the list on 2026-10-05 by a BoF proponent, Mirja Kuehlewind ("VCON is considered a suitable record format and the audit group will work with the vcon group to ensure the specified format addresses the auditing needs.") and accepted on 2026-10-06 by a participant ("I'm OK with this wording"); Henk Birkholz proposed a variant in charter style.

On 2026-10-06 Sumit Ahuja wrote that he and Iman Schrock are writing an expected-record draft, "We are aiming for the -00 before the IETF 127 cutoff", which "declares which records each participant in a cross-domain chain is expected to produce, what each establishes, and when an absent one becomes a finding"; Schrock listed six conformance cases for it (declaration integrity, all slots filled, bounded observed absence, incomplete observation, wrong or ineligible record, late record) and the claim boundary "no qualifying record was found in the declared sources by the cutoff". No text of that draft was read; it was announced on the list.

Case 4 reads "An unavailable source, incomplete pagination or coverage, unresolved participant set, or open window produces an incomplete audit, never an absence finding." On 2026-10-07 the author of this map wrote a seventh case on the list at Schrock's request, "Wrong recipient or holder": a record produced by the right party but held only by another does not fill the slot, and if the declared holder's source cannot be checked the audit is incomplete; the example is the confirmation that an EU trader owes the consumer on a durable medium (Directive 2011/83/EU, Art. 8(7)), held only by the agent platform. a co-author of the draft replied the same day that the case "catches a distinction the six cases do not state clearly" and asked for it to be written in the same shape; the draft text has not been read.

The text of PR #61 adds to deliverable 2: "For interaction records - the expectation is that the group will leverage the VCON format. Extensions to VCON to add data needed to model AI agent conversations will happen within the VCON group, and AUDIT will reference and (as needed) introduce profiles or use cases for it." On 2026-10-07 Henri Sirkkavaara proposed case 8, "Declined action": "A slot for an action that was refused before it ran is filled by the decider's signed refusal bound to that action."; on 2026-10-08 Iman Schrock replied "the refusal should satisfy the decision slot, not fill the downstream effect slot", and that a verified refusal "can make that effect slot not applicable when the declaration names refusal as a terminal condition." On 2026-10-07 Pavel Izmaylov posted the individual draft draft-izmaylov-agent-permission-receipts: the principal signs a permission with a passkey that carries "limits a machine can check" and conditions, and a verifier states for each recorded action whether it stayed within that permission.

Schrock replied on 2026-10-08 comparing it with draft-schrock-ep-authorization-receipts-13 and draft-schrock-ep-bounded-capability-receipts-06 and suggested a comparison in Section 11 of the draft. On 2026-09-30 Liu Chunchi (Peter) wrote on the list "Let me know if you want me to share some of our practices on EDR and Gateways during any interim or future meetings.", signing "Peter" without an affiliation; on 2026-10-08 the lead editor of the expected-record draft answered "Peter, thank you for offering text, and yes please." and Liu wrote that the architecture draft and the charter should "identify the necessary PEPs that should be covered first, rather than jumping into defining the record entries directly". On 2026-10-08, 13:56 UTC, the lead editor of the expected-record draft wrote "Both cases go into the -00." On 2026-10-11 (14:07 UTC) Lars Kroehl wrote a ninth case, "Refusal with an undetermined cause": "The slot is filled by a record that states whether a refusal came from a check that ran and failed, or from a check that could not be completed.", adding "AAE as published collapses both causes into a single DENY, so this case describes a gap in my own specification as much as anywhere else." Iman Schrock answered the same day (his text read as quoted in the lead editor's reply) that where the declaration requires grounds, "a single DENY cannot tell us whether revocation was established or the status check could not finish", and the lead editor of the expected-record draft wrote at 17:43 UTC "This goes into the -00 as Case 9, just like Iman put it.
