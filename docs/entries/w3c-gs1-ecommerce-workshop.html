Relevance

The workshop covers merchants' context, legal accountability, protocols, trust and ownership, product data, identity and authorisation, and payment; position statements (issues #10 to #34 and #45) are each the statement of its author. On seller authority and product identity, position statement #31 by Sven Böckelmann (benelog) says "Resolution says where the data is; a signature says who signed it.

Neither says who may speak for the product."; #21 (GS1 Digital Link) is related. On agent identity, delegation and consent, statements include #22 (FIDO Alliance Payments TWG, which says AP2 and Verifiable Intent are "now under review within the PTWG") and #26 (Skyfire).
