Relevance

The AP2 mandates extension says businesses must "embed a cryptographic signature in checkout responses, proving the terms (price, line items) are authentic" and platforms provide mandates "proving the user explicitly authorized the specific checkout state and funds transfer". The Order page calls the entity "the authoritative latest state of the order at the time of retrieval or delivery", where webhook payloads "be signed by the business and verified by the platform". Terms travel in policies[] (PR #572, merged 2026-07-23), where the business states rules such as a refund window; the PR says "Policies never force display." On agent identity, the signatures page (PR #483, merged 2026-07-03) says a dual-audience signature "is the RECOMMENDED path for integrators" who want interoperability with Web Bot Auth verifiers; that interoperability is request-scoped.

A food domain (dev.ucp.food.checkout, food.fulfillment) was proposed on 2026-10-03 for early feedback (PR #897, opened by a participant "On behalf of the entire Food Domain Tech Council"; its label on 2026-10-05 was status:needs-triage and on 2026-10-09 and 2026-10-13 is status:under-review, with gov:needs-tc-review and 12 comments on 2026-10-13).

A proposal to authenticate the platform client through attestation (issue #852, opened 2026-09-22) was described by its author on 2026-10-04 as "now ready for Technical Council review". Issue #375 (2026-05-29) declined external place identifiers in the core schema: "Adding specialized reconciliation mappings to the core schema is a premature optimization." The Common capability dev.ucp.common.ask was merged on 2026-10-07 (PR #538; commit a41a2dc).

Its page says "The Ask capability provides natural-language Q&A about a Business's resources, policies, and services." and "ask is the open-question complement to UCP's structured commerce capabilities". Two rules of its text: "A Business MUST NOT change the state of any resource exposed through another UCP capability or extension in response to an ask request." and "An answer is indicative, not authoritative."; where an answer conflicts with the representation returned by the capability that owns the resource, "that representation is authoritative and the Platform MUST prefer it", and "A Platform MUST NOT treat an answer as the binding disclosure for a safety, allergen, or regulatory claim." The page lists as typical use cases product questions ("fit, materials, compatibility, comparisons"), policy questions ("returns, shipping, warranty, refunds"), FAQ and how-to ("store hours, shipping timelines, accepted payment methods") and clarification about a specific resource, grounded in an identifier.

The description of the pull request, not the specification, lists "policies, fit and suitability, materials, compatibility, amenities, support information, and Business-specific knowledge". The merge adds docs/specification/common/ask/ (index, Representational State Transfer (REST) and MCP bindings), docs/specification/shopping/ask/index.md and source/schemas/common/ask.json; text read at commit f2206e6 (the head of main on 2026-10-09; on 2026-10-13 main is at 9756aa9, and a git diff of the two commits shows no change to the overview, AP2 mandates, signatures, order, ask and lodging pages cited here).

Pull request #811 (opened 2026-09-07 by a participant, open; among its labels on 2026-10-09 status:stale-review and gov:needs-tc-review; no comment or review; state and labels unchanged on 2026-10-13) proposes an optional offers[] array on the catalog variant "so one catalog can list a product sold by several different merchants and send each to the right seller's checkout". Each offer names its seller by seller.domain, the Merchant of Record, with the seller's own item_id and an authoritative price; the proposed schema text describes the domain as "The seller's canonical host: a bare hostname without scheme, path, port, or trailing slash" and adds "A Platform resolves this host to the seller's UCP profile at /.well-known/ucp and verifies its signing keys via authority binding." The overview page uses "canonical" for the host that serves a schema (lines 158 to 164); which of the bare and the www host is canonical for a profile is not found in the overview page at f2206e6 nor in the five files of the pull request (2026-10-09).

The overview page says, in Profile Requirements, Hosting, item 2, "Profile endpoints MUST NOT use redirects (3xx)." (line 1466) and, in the rules for any URL dereferenced during identity resolution, item 2, "Implementations MUST NOT follow redirects (3xx)." (line 1514); line 182 applies the same rule to schema URLs (file at commit f2206e6 of 2026-10-07; unchanged at 9756aa9 on 2026-10-13).

Items 3 and 4 of Hosting ask for a Cache-Control with public and a max-age of at least 60 seconds, and for a validator (ETag or Last-Modified). The profile rule first appears in commit 0426800 of 2026-02-17, "feat: signing for UCP requests & responses (#156)".
