# Contributing

Corrections and additions are accepted when they rest on public sources.

## What a proposal states

1. The entry id (the file name in `entries/`, without `.yaml`).
2. The exact sentence to change, copied from the entry.
3. The public URL of the source.
4. The date on which you read it.
5. A quote from the source, 40 words at most.
6. Disclosure: your role in the work you propose (author, maintainer, chair, contributor) and any employer or commercial interest in it, or "none".

An issue with the "Correction" template carries these fields.

## Rules of the map

- Committed, not predicted: "Committed next" lists only deliverables, events or intentions declared by the responsible body.
- No proposals in the entries: gaps and overlaps are recorded as facts.
- A participant's statement is never presented as the position of the venue.
- A negative is written as "not found in [corpus, date]".

## Proposals by third parties

A proposal made by a third party goes to the Proposals appendix only if it is public, and is listed with its author, its date and its state.

## Decisions

The author decides what is published and dates every change.

Numbered versions go out on Mondays. Proposals verified by Friday evening enter the following Monday; later ones move to the next week. A Monday with no change to citable content produces no new version.
